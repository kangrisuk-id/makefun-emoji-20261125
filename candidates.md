# Candidates — One Emoji, One Smile

## Brief

Pick exactly one emoji most likely to make a stranger smile. The judge compares two emojis at a time and values: (1) warm/friendly to a stranger, (2) warmth at first glance, (3) universality, (4) chosen rather than default, (5) right for a stranger.

## Shortlist

The strongest three after removing defaults, intimacy risks, and vague symbols:

1. **🥹 U+1F979 Face Holding Back Tears** — tender and modern; feels chosen. Risk: reads as moved/emotional more than immediately smile-making.
2. **🌻 U+1F33B Sunflower** — sunny and universally friendly; clearly chosen. Risk: warm, but less playful than a cute character.
3. **🐣 U+1F423 Hatching Chick** — cute, gently funny, warm, and unexpected; easy to read as a small cheerful hello.

## Criteria comparison

| Criterion | 🥹 | 🌻 | 🐣 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Warm/friendly, likely to make a stranger smile | Warm and tender, but can read as emotional rather than smile-first | Warm and friendly; pleasant smile | Warm, friendly, and directly smile-inducing through gentle cuteness |
| 2. Warmth at first glance | Kind/tender, needs a beat | Kind/sunny immediately | Kind and gently funny immediately |
| 3. Universality | Mostly universal, but emotion nuance varies | Very universal across ages/cultures | Very universal; baby animals read well everywhere |
| 4. Chosen rather than default | Clearly chosen | Clearly chosen | Clearly chosen; not a smiley default |
| 5. Right for a stranger | Safe from stranger | Safe from stranger | Safe from stranger; no intimacy/sarcasm/flirtation |

## Decision

**Winner: 🐣 (U+1F423, Hatching Chick)**. Against 🥹, it wins on criterion 1 because it is more directly likely to make a stranger smile, while 🥹 leans tender/moved. Against 🌻, it also wins on criterion 1 because its gentle cuteness gives an immediate smile with a touch of playful warmth, while staying just as universal, chosen, and stranger-appropriate.
